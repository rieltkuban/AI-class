# What works in 2026 short-form (research notes)

Sources are mostly practitioner blogs and tool vendors; their percentages are marketing, not data. The tactics below
are the ones several independent sources agree on, plus Apple's published motion principles.

## Retention
- The first 3 s decide the view: state the payoff before the context. (sparklineusa "MrBeast method", joyspace)
- Reset attention regularly with new angles, graphics, text — every few seconds. (sparklineusa)
- A kinetic emphasis around 2–4 s helps against early drop-off. (kreateflo, influencers-time)
- Overuse backfires: bouncing every syllable adds clutter; animate key words only, 1–2 typefaces. (influencers-time)
- Progress bars, UI-style overlays, minimal animated icons are the 2026 motion staples. (offshoreclipping, pixflow)

## Captions
- Phrase-level chunks synced to speech beat full static lines. (influencers-time)
- Hormozi style (heavy caps, key word highlighted, word by word) and MrBeast style (huge caps, spoken word in accent)
  are the US references; in 2026 a quieter "dynamic minimalism" keeps word-by-word captions and fast cuts but drops
  emoji and SFX clutter. (joyspace, ascynd, clipspeed, valmera)
- People watch muted: big readable text carries the message. (graphicdesignjunction)

## Look
- Liquid glass (iOS 26 design language): transparency, refraction, a light catching the edge — the premium look of
  2026 templates. (videobolt, autoae)
- Vertical-first composition, not a cropped horizontal layout. (graphicdesignjunction)

## Apple motion principles (WWDC "Designing Fluid Interfaces", "Principles of Great Design")
- Springs, critically damped by default; bounce only for momentum ("thrown" objects).
- Spatial consistency: enter and exit along the same path; anchor to the source.
- Speed reads better with a little motion blur / stretch than with a sharp streak.
- Typography: negative tracking on large display text, hierarchy by weight + size + leading.
- Avoid slow looping oscillations near 0.2 Hz and abrupt brightness jumps.

## motion-reel studio rules (@iishka26 plugin)
- The frame on the downbeat is the hit; a push shows both shots, never empty frames between.
- Zooms in log space (`s = a·(b/a)^p`).
- Banned defaults: centered title on gradient, everything fading in, glow on UI chrome, generic particles.
- Something new every 2–4 s; flash budget 1–2 per film.
- Critique loop on contact sheets, score ≥ 8 on hook, readability, motion, variety, brand, sync.
